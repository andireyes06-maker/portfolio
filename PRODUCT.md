## Product Principles

1. Evidence over claims — every stated fact traces to a real, verifiable
   project; unconfirmed details stay marked "coming soon" rather than
   invented.
2. Depth over breadth — a small number of real, well-documented projects
   beats padding with thin ones.
3. Separate identity from adjacent work — never blur this portfolio's
   authorship with Ninie's design portfolio, even though they share a
   client history.
4. Honest empty states — missing assets (screenshots, portrait, social
   links) are shown as clearly-labeled placeholders, never faked.
